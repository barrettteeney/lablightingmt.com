Disconnect

To disconnect Lab Lighting Bot from QuickBooks Online, sign in to QuickBooks, open Settings, go to connected apps, and disconnect Lab Lighting Bot. You can also revoke access in your Intuit account security settings, or email info@lablightingmt.com and ask us to disconnect it. After it is disconnected, we delete the stored sign-in tokens on our workstation, and the assistant can no longer access the Lab Lighting company file.
