About these terms

These terms cover your use of lablightingmt.com and how Lab Lighting LLC ("Lab Lighting", "we") quotes and installs permanent lighting. The project terms below summarize our Permanent Lighting Installation Agreement. If you hire us, the agreement you sign, together with your lighting plan and estimate, is the full and final agreement and controls over anything on this page.

Quotes, measurements and design previews

Estimates are priced per linear foot installed. Measurements come from aerial data and photos and are confirmed on site at the design visit. If the confirmed footage differs, the price is adjusted at the same per-foot rate, and we get your written approval of the new total before installing. Design previews are illustrations to help you picture your lights.

Scope of work

We supply and install permanent LED lighting: the lights, mounting track color-matched to your trim, power supply and controller, and app setup, along the runs shown on your lighting plan and estimate. Optional add-ons are included only if you select and initial them.

Deposit and payment

  • A deposit of 50% of the contract price is due no later than 14 days before your scheduled install date. We order materials and hold your date once the deposit is received.
  • If the deposit arrives after that deadline, we may move your installation to our next available date.
  • The remaining balance (50%, plus or minus any approved change) is due when installation is complete.
  • Lab Lighting keeps ownership of the installed system until the contract price is paid in full.

Scheduling

Install dates are estimates. We may reschedule for weather, unsafe roof or access conditions, or material delays, and we'll tell you as soon as possible. A typical installation takes about one day.

What we ask of you

  • Safe access to your property and the work areas on install day.
  • A working exterior or garage outlet near the planned controller location. New circuits or outlets are not included and must be done by a licensed electrician.
  • Any HOA or other approvals your property requires, before installation.
  • Wi-Fi access for app setup if you want app control.

Warranty and service

The lights, track and controller carry the manufacturer's parts warranty, under the manufacturer's terms. We warrant our installation workmanship for 1 year from the install date; during that year, defective parts and install-related failures are repaired at no charge. A trip fee of $125 to $175 applies to service calls for app help, resets, customer-caused issues, and anything outside the workmanship year. Warranties do not cover damage from storms, ice or snow loads, impact, roof or gutter work by others, power surges, or changes made by anyone other than Lab Lighting.

Your home and existing conditions

Track is fastened to the fascia or trim with small fasteners; we never penetrate your roof. We use reasonable care and are responsible for damage caused by our own negligence. We are not responsible for existing conditions, such as rotted or loose fascia. If we find one, we stop work on that section and talk with you before going further.

Changes

Changes to scope, footage or price must be agreed in writing (email or text is fine) by both parties. Added runs are priced at your agreement's per-foot rate unless agreed otherwise.

Cancellation and refunds

  • You may cancel within three business days of signing for a full refund, as described in the Notice of Cancellation included with your agreement.
  • After that, you may cancel in writing before installation begins. We refund your deposit minus the cost of any materials ordered specifically for your job that we cannot return.
  • If we cannot install within 30 days after the scheduled install date for reasons within our control, you may cancel for a full refund.
