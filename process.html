How It Works

From first message to lights on.

Here's exactly what happens after you reach out. The images below are the real plan and preview we made for a Flathead Valley home, with the address and prices hidden.

01
Within one business day

You reach out. We get in touch.

Fill out the form below, or call or text (406) 955-3400. A real person, not a call center, gets back to you to learn about your home and what you'd like lit.

Hi! I'd love to get permanent lights on our house before the holidays.
Thanks for reaching out! We'd be glad to help. Which parts of the house would you want lit: just the front, or the whole roofline?
Mostly the front and the garage.
02
Before anyone climbs a ladder

We measure your roof from above.

We order a satellite measurement report of your home. Every eave, gable and roof edge is measured to the foot, so your plan and price are built on real numbers instead of guesses.

03
Sent with your estimate

Your lighting plan, and your home at night.

You get a map of your home showing every run we'd light and its length, with optional add-ons marked separately so you can choose. We also show you a preview of your own house at night, so you know exactly what you're getting before you decide.

04
Sign online, no paperwork

Approve your estimate and reserve your date.

Your written estimate lists every run, its footage and its price. Approve it online, and a 50% deposit reserves your install date and orders your track, color-matched to your trim. Before install day we confirm the measurements and trim color in person. The balance isn't due until your lights are up and working.

05
About two weeks after your deposit

Your lights go up.

Most homes take a single day, weather permitting. Track fastens to the fascia or soffit (never through your roof), wiring stays hidden, and before we leave we set up the app on your phone and build your first scenes with you.
