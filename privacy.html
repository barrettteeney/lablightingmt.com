Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Who we are

LAB Lighting is the business name of Lab Lighting LLC, a permanent exterior lighting installer based in Kalispell, Montana. You can reach us at info@lablightingmt.com or (406) 955-3400.

What we collect

When you request an estimate, design visit or information through our website, a Facebook or Instagram lead form, a phone call, text or email, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your home address, so we can measure and quote your property
  • Details you share about your home and the lighting you want
  • Basic website usage data, such as pages viewed and the ad you clicked, collected through cookies and advertising tools like the Meta Pixel

How we use it

  • To contact you about your request, schedule a measurement and prepare your quote
  • To install, support and service your lighting system
  • To send occasional messages about your project or our services, which you can opt out of at any time
  • To understand which of our ads and pages are useful, and to show our ads to people likely to be interested

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the business (for example our scheduling and customer management software, our email and phone providers, and advertising platforms such as Meta), with our lighting manufacturer when needed to register a warranty or order parts for your home, or when required by law.

Calls and texts

If you give us your phone number, you agree that we may call or text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving them.

How long we keep it

We keep customer and quote records for as long as needed to serve you, honor warranties and meet tax and legal requirements. You can ask us to delete information we no longer need.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or opt out of marketing, by emailing info@lablightingmt.com. You can also control cookies through your browser settings and manage ad preferences in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

Children

Our services are for homeowners and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
