Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Who we are

Lab Lighting is the business name of Lab Lighting LLC, a permanent architectural lighting installer based in Kalispell, Montana. This policy explains how we handle information in two places: our internal QuickBooks Online integration, called Lab Lighting Bot, and information people submit through our public website.

A shorter website-only policy also lives at lablightingmt.com/privacy.html. This page is the policy we provide for the Lab Lighting Bot integration.

Lab Lighting Bot, in short

Lab Lighting Bot is a private tool used only by Lab Lighting LLC. It connects our own internal assistant to our own QuickBooks Online company so we can work with estimates, invoices, customers, items, and reports. No outside users sign up for it. We do not sell it, and we do not connect it to any other company's books.

What QuickBooks data is accessed, and why

When an authorized owner connects Lab Lighting's QuickBooks company, the integration can read and write accounting records in that company file, including:

  • Customer records
  • Estimates and invoices
  • Items and related lists
  • Standard financial reports
  • Other accounting records needed to prepare or update those documents

We use that access only to operate Lab Lighting's business — for example, drafting an estimate, checking a customer record, or reviewing a report. Intuit also required a Payments permission on the developer app. We do not use this integration to process card payments or take payment information through the app.

Where it is stored

Intuit's QuickBooks Online service remains the system of record for the company file. The integration runs Intuit's official open-source QuickBooks Online server on a private cloud workstation we control. Sign-in tokens needed to stay connected are stored in a restricted file on that workstation. While an owner is using the assistant, relevant records may be shown in that private session so the work can be done. We do not operate a separate public database of QuickBooks data, and we do not sell or rent this information.

Sharing

We do not sell QuickBooks data. We do not share it with other businesses or the public. Access is limited to Lab Lighting's owners (and staff we authorize) and to the internal assistant acting for them. Intuit continues to host the company file under Intuit's own terms. We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention

QuickBooks company records stay in QuickBooks for as long as we need them to serve customers, honor warranties, and meet tax and legal requirements. Connection tokens are kept only while the integration is authorized. When we disconnect or revoke the app, we delete the stored tokens and the assistant can no longer access the company file.

How to disconnect or revoke access

An owner can disconnect Lab Lighting Bot from QuickBooks Online at any time. In QuickBooks, open Settings, find connected apps, and disconnect Lab Lighting Bot, or revoke access from your Intuit account security settings. You can also email us at info@lablightingmt.com and ask us to disconnect it. More detail is on our disconnect page.

Website visitors and quote forms

If you request a measurement or quote on lablightingmt.com, or contact us by phone, text, or email, we may collect the information you give us — typically your name, phone number, email address if you provide one, home address, and notes about your home or the lighting you want. We use that to follow up, measure, and prepare a written quote, and to serve you if you hire us.

The website may use cookies or similar tools needed to run the site and understand basic use of the pages. We do not sell the personal information you submit. You can ask to see, correct, or delete personal information we hold, or opt out of marketing, by emailing us.

This website uses the Meta Pixel to measure how our ads perform and to show you relevant ads. Meta may collect cookie and device data when you visit. You can opt out in your Facebook ad preferences at https://www.facebook.com/adpreferences, and you can also control cookies in your browser settings.

Children

Our services are for homeowners and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
