A-06FAQGeneral notes

Straight answers.

Short and specific. If yours is not here, call or text (406) 955-3400.

N-01Is this just holiday lighting?

No. It is year-round architectural lighting first: warm white on your roofline, every night. Holidays, game days and events are a feature that comes with it.

N-02Does it show during the day?

The track is color-matched to your soffit and fascia trim. Finishes follow Gentek and Kaycan trim colors (GT Pebble 559, for example), and custom colors are possible. It runs along the outside edge of the soffit, not the wall, so it disappears by day.

N-03How does it handle Montana snow and ice?

It is rated to −40° and sealed against water. The aluminum track also shields the LEDs from ice, snow load and UV.

N-04Will my HOA allow it?

Usually yes, because the track is color-matched and nearly invisible in daylight and the lighting can be set to a single warm white. If your HOA requires approval, we will provide track color samples and specifications for your submission.

N-05What is the warranty?

Two parts. Celebright covers parts for 10 years, and that coverage transfers with the home. Lab Lighting covers workmanship for 1 year.

N-06How do I control it?

From the app. Pick a scene, set a schedule, done. No monthly fees.

N-07How much power does it use?

Very little. The system runs on 24 volts and uses less energy than a single 60-watt bulb for every hundred lights.

N-08What does the estimate include?

Everything. Every estimate is all-inclusive: lights, track, controller, color-matched install and app setup.

Nothing is added on top. No hidden fees, no extra charges.

N-09How is my estimate calculated?

We measure every run of your roofline from aerial photos, street photos and roof pitch. Each run rounds up to the whole foot and the total rounds up to the next 10 ft. That length sets your estimated total, shown as a range in the pricing table. Over 300 ft is a custom quote. No discounts.

A 50% deposit is due 14 days before install.
