The System

Engineered for Montana winters.

Low-voltage 24V lighting that plugs into a standard exterior outlet — typically no electrician, no trenching, no rewiring. The whole system draws less power than a single 60-watt bulb for every hundred lights.

Each bulb is a single point of light with a dedicated warm-white LED and 90+ CRI color rendering, so white reads as true warm white with no color fringing. Everything is sealed against the weather, and the aluminum track shields the LEDs from snow, ice and UV. The system is designed and built in Canada by Celebright, one of the few manufacturers that makes its own bulbs, control box, track and app, and tests them for real winters.

WHAT MOVES YOUR PRICE

Four things, and you can see all of them

Footage is the big one, then height and access, then corner and peak count, then track profile. A 150-foot single story with clean runs prices very differently from 150 feet broken across six dormers — same footage, twice the work. Ask us to walk the tiers with you at the measurement; there is no hidden math.

PAYMENT

Half down, half when it works

A 50% deposit reserves your install date and covers your color-matched materials. The balance is due the day the system is finished and running — not before.

WHAT IT COSTS TO RUN

About $8 a year in electricity

Each bulb draws 0.3 watts. A typical 150-foot roofline is roughly 225 bulbs — about 68 watts for the whole house, less than one old porch bulb. Running five hours a night, every night, that is roughly 120 kWh a year, or about eight dollars at Flathead Electric's current residential rate.

Questions

Straight answers.

Will it look like Christmas lights in July?

No. In daylight the track is color-matched to your fascia and reads as trim. At night the default is a refined warm white that lights the architecture. Color scenes are there when you want them and off the rest of the time.

How much power does it use?

Very little. The system runs on 24 volts and uses less energy than a single 60-watt bulb for every hundred lights.

Is there a monthly fee or subscription?

No. The app is free and there is nothing to renew. You pay for the install once.

Does it work with Alexa or Google?

Yes. It works with Amazon Alexa, Google Home and IFTTT, so it can react to things like a doorbell camera or a game-day calendar.

What happens if I sell the house?

The lights stay with the home and the 10-year parts warranty transfers to the new owner. Buyers tend to see it as a finished, well-kept exterior.

Does it damage my roof or fascia?

Track mounts to fascia or soffit with screws. We do not penetrate roofing or shingles. Mounting holes are small and are a normal part of any exterior installation.

Do I need an electrician?

Usually not. The system is low-voltage and plugs into a standard exterior outlet. If your home needs a new outlet we will tell you before the job and coordinate a licensed electrician.

Where does the control box go?

Most often inside the garage, out of the weather and easy to reach. We confirm the location with you before install day.

Can I control it when I'm away?

Yes. The system is cloud connected, so you can change scenes or schedules from the app or a web browser anywhere with internet.

What if a bulb fails in year six?

Parts are covered by a 10-year manufacturer warranty that transfers to the next owner if you sell, and we handle the claim. Individual bulbs are replaceable, so a failure is a small fix rather than a new system. Our workmanship is covered for one year; service visits outside those terms are a flat trip fee plus parts, quoted before we come out.

Can it survive Montana winters?

It is rated to −40° and sealed against water. The aluminum track also shields the LEDs from ice, snow load and UV, which is a large part of why permanent systems outlast clipped-on lights.

Can you install in the winter?

Sometimes, weather permitting. We do not work on icy or snow-covered roofs, and we reschedule when it drops below about 5°F or the wind picks up, because cold vinyl siding and wire insulation crack easily. The best window is late summer through mid-November. Winter customers can sign in the off-season, lock in pricing, and install in early spring.

What if I replace my roof or paint later?

We remove and reinstall the system around roofing or painting work for a quoted flat rate. Nothing gets thrown away.

Will my HOA allow it?

Usually yes, because the track is color-matched and nearly invisible in daylight and the lighting can be set to a single warm white. If your HOA requires approval, we will provide track color samples and specifications for your submission.

How long does installation take?

Most homes are a single day. Larger properties and multi-zone packages may take two.
