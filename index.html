What We Do

Architectural lighting, not Christmas lights.

Permanent lighting is a finish on your home, like gutters or trim. Ours disappears during the day and reveals the architecture at night — every night of the year, not six weeks in December.

RESIDENTIAL

Rooflines & Eaves

  • 11 track profiles
  • 100+ track colors
  • Hidden wire runs

About

Locally owned, valley based.

Lab Lighting is a small, locally owned company in Kalispell. The people who measure your home are the people who install it, and the ones who pick up the phone when you have a question three years from now.

We chose one system and learned it completely rather than carrying five brands we half understand. Every install is straight track, hidden wire, sealed penetrations and a clean site — because every roofline we finish is the only advertising that matters.
